Resonant Geodes

Home My Mods About Me

Resonant Geodes is all about different kinds of Geodes that can be found underground, and the ability to obtain theoretically infinite resources from them.

Content

Resource Geodes

  • Made of Geode Crystals
  • Contains a Budding Geode Core which grows more of them

Geode Drill

  • Built around a Budding Geode Core
  • Uses Crystal Aura to produce the Geode's Resource Crystals at a fixed rate
  • Crystals can only be extracted from the Bottom side

Crystal Diffuser

  • Diffuses inserted Geode Crystals into a Global Aura to fuel Geode Drills
  • The Lava used to build it is not consumed by operation
